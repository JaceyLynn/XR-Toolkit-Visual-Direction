Landing page visuals, round 4

From the client’s feedback on round 3: the rhizome is the direction, with no stars or sky, nodes of light that twinkle, and warm earth colours rather than a tech look.

Nodes of Light

Two rhizome networks grow in from opposite corners of a warm, dark, grainy ground, where the colours flow slowly underneath, drawing curved, forking paths from node to node. On the first screen they grow toward the headline and touch inside it, leaving a soft glow where they meet, then keep spreading until the ground is full. Each node lights up when a path reaches it and then twinkles at its own pace, with a slow breath and an occasional glint; nodes with more links glow a little more. Paths never disappear; once drawn they settle to a softer glow. Moving the pointer steers nearby growth toward it. Lower down, the view moves much closer in: the first network drifts out of focus and a nearer rhizome grows after your pointer, starting just behind it and branching toward wherever it moves. Hovering a way in sends a branch from each network to meet under that button, joined by a bridge of light.

Main elements
  • A warm, matte ground with a slow, gentle flow of colour under a fine paper-like grain, soft mottling and faint out-of-focus roots, in place of the sky
  • Two rhizome networks growing from the lower-left and upper-right corners, meeting in the headline with no single hub
  • Nodes of light that twinkle, light up when a path arrives, and glow more where the network is busiest
  • Growth that bends toward the pointer on the first screen and follows it closely on the lower half, and both networks meeting at whichever way in you hover

Colour palette

Each palette comes from one of the reference palettes: Late Harvest (grasslands on grape fizz), Poppy Dusk (wild poppies and thyme) and Gilded Iris (old gold and royal purple on gunmetal).

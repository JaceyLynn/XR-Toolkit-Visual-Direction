Visual style direction

Murmuration at sunrise

A Savoy blue to soft purple sky with clouds drifting down, their undersides lit faint coral, and a 3D flock of flat dots in warm white and dusty plum with a few sunrise coral sparks. At rest the flock swirls like starlings at dusk. Hover over the first screen and it gathers into a great egret seen from below that flaps, glides and turns as it follows you, while the coral sparks flicker across its wings. Lower down, the flock circles whichever way in you point at.

Main elements
  • 3D flock of white and plum dots that moves like a murmuration
  • Hover to gather the flock into a flying great egret, with flickering coral sparks
  • The ↓ and the two ways in light up like a sunrise on hover, and the flock circles the button
  • Clouds drifting down, as if the flock is climbing
  • Dot array around the pointer, and a short line beside ↓ on hover
  • FA-1 title and buttons, Hauora Sans text

Starry Aurora

A warm, deep blue night sky seen from below. As you scroll, the view tilts up until the aurora is overhead and the two ways in sit between its curtains. The aurora bends toward your pointer, and constellations start drawing themselves as soon as the page opens.

Main elements
  • Warm deep blue night sky with a 3D aurora that turns overhead as you scroll
  • Dot grid and an aurora that follows your pointer
  • Constellations that form on their own or as you join stars
  • Nova Square and Panamera type
